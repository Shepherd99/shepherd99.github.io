About & Learning Philosophy

I am Christopher Chinedu, a dedicated cybersecurity practitioner focused on building rigorous, foundational expertise across defensive operations and threat intelligence. I believe that effective defensive security starts with a deep, intuitive comprehension of how protocols behave at the wire level and how operating systems enforce boundaries.
